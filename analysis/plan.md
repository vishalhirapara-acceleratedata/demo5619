# Monthly Sales Trends — Analysis Plan

1. **Locate and inspect the sales source data** — identify the table/view holding sales, its grain, and the date and amount columns.
2. **Aggregate to monthly grain** — sum/group sales by month (and any key dimension) to produce monthly totals.
3. **Review trends and anomalies** — compare month-over-month change, highlight spikes/dips, and note any data-quality caveats.
